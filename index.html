Lemon Lentil Soup serves 4 · 40 min

ingredients

  • 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 5 cups vegetable stock
  • juice of 1 lemon
  • olive oil, salt, pepper, cumin

method

  1. Soften the onion and carrots in olive oil for 8 minutes.
  2. Add garlic and cumin, stir for a minute.
  3. Add lentils and stock. Simmer 20 minutes until soft.
  4. Blend part of the soup, add lemon, season, and serve warm.

note: this is a sample recipe. Replace it with your own.
